# Official Notice: Structural Reorganization and Policy Enforcement

Effective immediately, the Apich Organization is initiating a mandatory operational restructuring. This decision follows significant legal pressure and unavoidable internal conflicts that have necessitated a shift in our organizational trajectory.

Most notably, these conflicts were precipitated by the betrayal of our established security policies by several members. We maintain a strict standard for memory safety, cryptographic integrity, and compliance; when these fundamental tenets were compromised, the individuals responsible for these actions departed from the organization.

## Impact on Projects and Operations

1. As a result of this internal realignment, the following changes are effective .Transition to Maintenance Mode: The majority of our software projects are shifting into a strict maintenance-only phase. We are halting new feature development to focus exclusively on essential stability.

2. Reduced Support Capacity: Due to the departure of involved members, our capacity for rapid response has diminished. Users should expect significant delays in bug report resolution and security vulnerability auditing.

3. Security Accountability: We are conducting a thorough audit of all past contributions associated with the departed members. While we remain committed to our security-first philosophy, response times for incoming issues will be deferred until this audit is concluded.

## Continuity of Research

While our systems and software operations are undergoing this contraction, the Science & Research team remains fully operational and entirely unaffected by these events.

Our core mandate—advancing the frontiers of High-Performance Computing and Theoretical Physics—continues unabated. The integrity of our research remains our highest priority, and we will continue to focus our resources on these scientific objectives.

We appreciate the understanding of our community as we enforce these necessary standards and secure the future of our research initiatives.
